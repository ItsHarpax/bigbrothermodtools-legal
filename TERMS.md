# Terms of Use

Last updated October 15, 2026

bigbrothermodtools is a Reddit app made for the moderators of r/BigBrother. It schedules the subreddit's recurring posts, keeps the stickied comments on discussion threads up to date, and keeps a record of moderator actions.

## Using it

The app is installed by the moderators of r/BigBrother for that subreddit only. Its moderator tools can only be used by the subreddit's moderators. Everyone else sees only what it posts, like any other post or comment on the subreddit.

Using the app, or the subreddit it runs on, is also covered by Reddit's [User Agreement](https://redditinc.com/policies/user-agreement) and [Content Policy](https://redditinc.com/policies/content-policy).
