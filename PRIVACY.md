# Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

bigbrothermodtools is a set of moderation tools for the moderators of r/BigBrother. It replaces or adds to Reddit's own moderation features for the mod team, and only the subreddit's moderators can use it.

The app keeps the records it needs to moderate the subreddit, such as a log of moderator actions. When a post or comment is deleted, its text is removed from those records, and when an account is deleted, its username is removed too. Nothing the app keeps is sold or shared outside the r/BigBrother mod team, and the only thing it sends outside Reddit is short notices to the mod team's own Discord about the app's work.

The app runs on Reddit, and Reddit's [Privacy Policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy) also applies.

## Questions

Send a message to the r/BigBrother moderators through modmail.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
