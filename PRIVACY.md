# Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

bigbrothermodtools is a Reddit app used by the moderators of r/BigBrother to run the subreddit. This page explains what it stores and what it sends.

## What it stores

The app keeps its data in Reddit's own app storage for the subreddit. It stores:

- **The subreddit's mod log**, from the day the app was installed: what action a moderator took, when, on which post or comment, the author's username, the removal reason, and the title or text of the post or comment as it was when the action happened.
- **Scheduled posts**: the post templates the moderators set up, and a history of the posts the app has made.
- **The stickied comments** the app keeps on discussion threads, and the settings for them.
- **The body of any post the app makes**, so it can be shown and edited.

It does not keep a copy of the subreddit's posts and comments in general. It only keeps content that a moderator has acted on.

## When something is deleted

If you delete your own post or comment, the app removes its stored text from the mod log. The same happens when Reddit removes something for legal reasons. The record that a moderator acted on it stays, without the text.

## Where it goes

What the app stores is only shown to the r/BigBrother moderators, inside Reddit.

The one thing it sends outside Reddit is short notices to the mod team's Discord, if the moderators have set that up: when the app makes a post, when the feed discussion thread changes over, when it locks or unlocks a thread, and when something goes wrong. These contain post titles, links and a description of the problem, never anyone's post or comment text. The app doesn't send anything to any other website or service.

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't show ads, sell data, or share it with anyone outside the r/BigBrother mod team.
- It doesn't use cookies or track you.
- The mod log page inside the subreddit checks whether you are a moderator, using your Reddit username. That check isn't stored.

## Questions

Send a message to the r/BigBrother moderators through modmail.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
